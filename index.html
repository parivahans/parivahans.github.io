Overview

What Is Parivahan Sewa?

India's centralised digital gateway for all road transport citizen services, built and maintained by MoRTH and NIC.

For decades, every interaction with India's Regional Transport Offices demanded physical visits, stacks of paperwork, and full working days lost in queues. Parivahan Sewa — launched by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways in partnership with the National Informatics Centre — permanently changed that reality.

The platform operates through two flagship sub-portals. VAHAN manages everything related to motor vehicles: registration, road tax, fitness certificates, permits, and ownership transfers. Sarathi handles everything related to the driver: learner's licences, permanent DLs, renewals, duplicates, and test slot bookings.

Both systems draw from and contribute to a single unified national transport database, ensuring records remain consistent regardless of which state or RTO you interact with. Alongside these, eChallan digitises traffic fine collection, while the mParivahan app stores your RC and DL as legally valid documents on your phone.

Scale: Parivahan Sewa processes tens of millions of transactions annually — spanning new vehicle registrations, licence applications, tax collections, and challan settlements — making it one of the largest citizen-facing government digital platforms in South Asia.

How to Check RC Status Online

Your Registration Certificate details — validity, insurance, PUC, fitness — are all in the national VAHAN database. Here's how to pull them up in under a minute.

01
Open the Portal

Go to parivahan.gov.in and click Online Services → Vehicles Related Services.

02
Click "Know Your Vehicle"

On the VAHAN services page, select Know Your Vehicle Details. You do not need to log in for this step.

03
Enter Registration Number

Type your vehicle number in the format MH01AB1234 (no spaces, no hyphens), then pass the captcha.

04
Review Your RC Details

The result page shows RC validity, insurance status, PUC expiry, fitness certificate date, and vehicle class instantly.

What You Can See
  • RC validity and expiry date
  • Current insurance policy status & expiry
  • PUC (Pollution Under Control) certificate validity
  • Fitness certificate date (commercial vehicles)
  • Vehicle class, fuel type, and make/model
  • Hypothecation (loan) details, if any
  • National Permit validity (transport vehicles)
Common RC Problems and Fixes
  • RC expired — renew online via VAHAN using Form 25
  • Insurance lapsed — buy a new policy and update via insurer
  • PUC expired — visit any PUC centre; updated in 24 hrs
  • Wrong address on RC — apply for address change on VAHAN
  • RC not found — contact your issuing RTO; data may not be migrated
  • Duplicate RC needed — apply online with Form 26

You can also download a soft copy of your RC directly from DigiLocker or through the mParivahan app — both are legally valid on the road.

Step-by-Step Guide

How to Apply for a Driving Licence Online

The complete two-stage Sarathi Parivahan process — from learner's licence to permanent DL.

Stage 1 — Learner's Licence (LL)

Completed entirely online. Valid 6 months — hold for at least 30 days before applying for a Permanent DL.

1

Open Sarathi & Select Your State

Navigate to sarathi.parivahan.gov.in. Select your home state from the dropdown — choosing correctly is essential to reaching the right RTO's system.

2

Choose "Apply for Learner Licence"

Select the Learner Licence option from the state menu. Log in with your mobile number or create an account linked to your Aadhaar for identity verification.

3

Fill the Application Form

Enter your full legal name, date of birth, address, blood group, and emergency contact. Every detail must exactly match your identity documents — discrepancies cause delays or rejection.

4

Upload Supporting Documents

Scan and upload clear copies of identity proof, address proof, age proof, and a recent passport-size photograph on a white background — within the portal's file size limits.

5

Pay the ₹200 Fee Online

Complete payment via UPI, net banking, or card. Download and save your payment acknowledgement — it is your reference for all future correspondence.

6

Clear the Traffic Knowledge Test

Most states offer the mandatory MCQ test within the Sarathi portal — covering road signs, traffic rules, and safe driving practices. Minimum passing score: 57%.

7

Receive Your Learner's Licence

Upon passing, your LL is generated digitally and sent to your registered email. Physical LL cards are dispatched to your address within a few working days where applicable.

Hold your LL for at least 30 days — and within its 6-month validity — before applying for a Permanent DL.

Stage 2 — Permanent Driving Licence

Requires one physical RTO visit for the practical test. DL dispatched by post within 7–14 working days.

1

Return to Sarathi & Select "Apply for DL"

Enter your LL number, date of birth, and complete OTP verification. Upload your LL copy, address proof, and a fresh photograph.

2

Pay ₹200 DL Fee + ₹300 Test Fee

Complete both payments online and download your official receipt before booking your test appointment.

3

Book Your RTO Test Appointment

Use the Sarathi slot-booking system to schedule your practical driving test. You can reschedule online up to 24 hours before your appointment.

Metro RTOs fill weeks in advance — try an adjoining district RTO for an earlier date.

4

Attend the RTO & Pass the Practical Test

Carry all original documents and your printed application receipt. After passing, your Permanent DL is dispatched to your registered address within 7–14 working days.

Eligibility Requirements

16 yrs — Gearless <50cc (parental consent)
18 yrs — All motorcycles, cars & LMVs
20 yrs — Commercial transport vehicles
Physical & mental fitness (Form 1 / 1A)
Pass traffic knowledge test

Documents Required

Identity Proof — Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID, or PAN
Address Proof — Aadhaar, Passport, or utility bill (<3 months)
Age Proof — Birth certificate or Class 10 marksheet
Passport-size Photograph on white background
Learner's Licence (for Permanent DL only)
Form 1 or 1A — fitness declaration / medical certificate

Traffic Fines

How to Check & Pay Your eChallan

Traffic challans in most Indian states are now issued exclusively in digital format. Clear yours in minutes, without a court visit.

01
Open the eChallan Portal

Go to parivahan.gov.in → Online Services → eChallan, or visit echallan.parivahan.gov.in directly.

02
Click "Challan Status"

Under "Check Online Services," click Challan Status to begin searching for pending fines.

03
Enter Your Identifier

Enter one of: your Challan Number, Driving Licence Number, or Vehicle Registration Number.

04
Review Challan Details

Click Get Details to retrieve all pending challans — offence type, date, issuing authority, and fine amount.

05
Pay Online Securely

Select the challan(s) and click Pay Now to settle via UPI, net banking, or card. Download your payment receipt.

⚠ Do Not Ignore Pending Challans

Unresolved eChallans block your RC renewal and attract escalated penalties. Check regularly, especially if you drive through camera-monitored corridors.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed answers to the questions most commonly asked by Indian vehicle owners and drivers.

Q1What is Parivahan Sewa and who manages it?
Parivahan Sewa is the Government of India's official unified platform for all road transport citizen services. It is developed and operated by the National Informatics Centre (NIC) on behalf of the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH). The platform serves all 36 states and UTs, connecting citizens, RTOs, traffic police, insurers, and transport operators through a shared national database.
Q2Can I complete the entire driving licence process without visiting an RTO?
Most of the process — application, document upload, fee payment, and the Learner's Licence knowledge test — can be completed entirely online via sarathi.parivahan.gov.in. However, the practical driving test for the Permanent DL still requires a physical RTO visit. A brief biometric visit is also needed for renewals and duplicates in most states.
Q3How do I check my RC status online?
Visit parivahan.gov.in and select Online Services → Vehicle Related Services → Know Your Vehicle Details. Enter your registration number (no spaces or hyphens) and complete the captcha. The page shows RC validity, insurance status, PUC expiry, fitness certificate date, and vehicle class. Full owner identity details are restricted to authorised agencies. You can also check the RC/DL status page at parivahan.gov.in/rcdlstatus.
Q4How do I verify vehicle owner details using just the registration number?
Visit parivahan.gov.in and use the "Know Your Vehicle Details" tool. Enter the registration number and complete the captcha. The system displays RC validity, insurance expiry, PUC status, and vehicle class. Full owner identity details are restricted to authorised agencies to protect citizen privacy.
Q5A challan has been wrongly issued against my vehicle — how do I dispute it?
Visit echallan.parivahan.gov.in, retrieve your challan details, and select "Dispose in Court" — this routes the matter to the appropriate traffic court. You may also email helpdesk-echallan [at] gov.in with your challan number, vehicle details, and supporting evidence such as GPS records or photographs.
Q6Is the mParivahan app accepted as a valid substitute for physical documents?
Yes — completely. Following the 2019 amendment to the Motor Vehicles Act, digital documents in mParivahan or DigiLocker are fully legally valid and must be accepted by all enforcement authorities across India. If a police officer refuses to accept your digital document, you may note their badge number and file a grievance with the state transport department.
Q7What is the difference between VAHAN and Sarathi?
VAHAN is vehicle-centric — managing RC issuance, road tax, fitness certificates, permits, hypothecation, and ownership transfers. Sarathi is driver-centric — managing learner's and permanent licences, renewals, duplicates, test bookings, and driving school accreditations. Both share a common national database under the Parivahan Sewa umbrella.
Q8How long does it take to receive the physical driving licence card?
After passing your driving test, your Permanent DL is typically dispatched within 7 to 14 working days via India Post Speed Post. Track dispatch status anytime on the Sarathi portal. If the card has not arrived within 30 days of the expected dispatch date, raise a grievance through Sarathi's online redressal system.
Q9Can I transfer a vehicle to another state through Parivahan Sewa?
Yes. Apply for a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the original state's RTO using Form 28 via VAHAN. Once issued, re-register with the destination state's RTO using Form 29 and Form 30, and pay the new state's road tax. Most states process NOCs digitally within 5–7 working days.
Q10What can I do if the Parivahan Sewa portal is down or showing errors?
Portal downtime is usually announced on the official parivahan.gov.in homepage. In most cases, retry after a few hours. For persistent issues, email the relevant helpdesk — helpdesk-vahan [at] gov.in for vehicle matters, helpdesk-sarathi [at] gov.in for DL matters. Include your application number, mobile number, and a screenshot of the error for faster resolution.
