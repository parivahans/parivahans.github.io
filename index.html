Parivahan Sewa Portal

Digital Transport Services by Ministry of Road Transport & Highways (MoRTH)

Parivahan Sewa is an official online portal launched by the Government of India to provide vehicle-related and driving licence services to citizens in a transparent and digital manner.

About Parivahan Sewa

Parivahan Sewa is a centralized transport service platform that connects Regional Transport Offices (RTOs) across India. The portal helps citizens access transport-related services without visiting RTO offices repeatedly.

Through this portal, users can apply for driving licences, register vehicles, check RC and DL status, download documents, apply for permits, and pay transport-related taxes and fees online.

Driving Licence Services

  • Apply for Learner Licence (LL)
  • Apply for Permanent Driving Licence (DL)
  • DL Renewal
  • Duplicate Driving Licence
  • Change of Address in DL
  • International Driving Permit (IDP)
  • Slot Booking for Driving Test

Applicants can complete most of the driving licence process online, including form submission, fee payment, and appointment booking.

Vehicle Related Services

  • New Vehicle Registration
  • Transfer of Ownership
  • Duplicate Registration Certificate (RC)
  • Address Change in RC
  • RC Renewal
  • Vehicle Hypothecation Entry / Termination
  • Vehicle Tax Payment
  • Fancy Number Booking
  • Fitness Certificate

Vehicle owners can manage all registration and ownership services digitally using Parivahan Sewa .

Permit & Transport Services

  • National Permit
  • State Permit
  • Temporary Permit
  • Goods Carrier Permit
  • Passenger Vehicle Permit
  • Permit Renewal

Check Application Status

Users can track the status of their applications online using:

  • Driving Licence Application Number
  • Vehicle Registration Number
  • Challan Number

Status tracking helps citizens stay updated without visiting the RTO office.

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Address Proof
  • Age Proof
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Medical Certificate (if applicable)
  • Vehicle Insurance
  • PUC Certificate

Benefits of Parivahan Sewa

  • Online services available 24×7
  • Reduced RTO visits
  • Transparent process
  • Digital payments
  • Nationwide uniform system
  • Time-saving and paperless

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is Parivahan Sewa an official government website?

Yes, Parivahan Sewa is operated by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH), Government of India.

Can I apply for DL completely online?

You can apply online, but biometric verification and driving test require physical visit to RTO.

Can RC and DL be downloaded online?

Yes, RC and DL can be downloaded through DigiLocker or mParivahan app.

Is Aadhaar mandatory?

Aadhaar is required for most services for identity verification.
